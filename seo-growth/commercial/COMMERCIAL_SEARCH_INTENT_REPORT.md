# Commercial search-intent research

**Research date:** 23 November 2026. The database contains exact suggested phrases in English, French and Arabic. The phrases are query hypotheses and translated equivalents, not measured demand. No numerical search-volume or organic-difficulty dataset was obtained. The “competition evidence” column summarizes public-page examples rather than an SEO difficulty score.

## Intent patterns

| Cluster | Buyer question | Evidence from reviewed search results | Opica readiness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | What does a site/app/software project cost? Is the quote understandable? | Chad sources show both public tiers and quote-after-scope. Regional FCFA website/app starting prices and international hourly ranges are public. | No Opica price. Answer with a transparent scoping method only after owner approval; do not claim cheapest/affordable. |
| Specialization | Does the developer use Flutter/React Native/FastAPI and have relevant work? | Portfolio lists those stacks and project examples; competitors also advertise apps, websites and custom tools. | Technology presence is supported; service availability, role, production quality and specialism need confirmation. |
| Speed | How soon will someone answer, and how soon can a project launch? | Tchaditech publicly claims response under 24 business hours; Albiher under 48 hours; competitor delivery tiers vary. | No Opica availability, monitoring or actual project durations documented. |
| Trust / warranty | Who fixes defects after launch? What is excluded? Who owns code? | Reviewed provider policies range from 15 to 90 days and distinguish defects from new features/third-party failures. | No Opica warranty, maintenance plan, SLA, IP terms or testimonials. |

## Local language and targeting notes

- French is the clearest language of public commercial competitor pages reviewed in Chad. French is likely an immediate research language, but actual customer-language and service coverage should be confirmed.
- Arabic equivalents in the CSV are linguistic suggestions. No Chad-specific Arabic search volume, SERP study or customer-language survey was available.
- English phrases align with international/remote buyer research. A separate English campaign should not imply cross-border availability until confirmed.
- City keywords such as N'Djamena/نجامينا require confirmed geographic service relevance. A Chad telephone prefix alone is not proof of business location.
- The current website language switch serves one URL. These keyword ideas should not be stuffed into hidden content or treated as crawlable locale pages. Existing SEO strategy says language-specific indexable URLs would require real translated routes.

## Measurement plan

1. Export Google Ads Keyword Planner estimates separately by country, language, network and date. Record ranges as reported, not fake precise volumes. Paid-ad competition is not organic difficulty.
2. After deployment/verification, use Search Console query, country, impression, click and landing-page data. Filter terms by commercial cluster and note URL/language limitations.
3. Ask qualified prospects what wording they use and record lost/won reason, project type, budget range and timing, with privacy safeguards.
4. Review whether prospective keywords actually map to a useful page. Keep unsupported topics (AI, warranties, “cheap,” “fastest”) research-only.

## Database interpretation

`COMMERCIAL_KEYWORD_DATABASE.csv` records suggested phrases, language, market, intent, service, proposed page, observed competition evidence, search-volume evidence, value and confidence. The search-volume field explicitly says “not measured”; confidence refers to the phrase's fit as a commercial-intent hypothesis, not demand. Do not generate volume or difficulty numbers from competitor count.
