## Project evidence checklist

For each portfolio item record: concept/demo/shipped status; client/internal status; author's exact role; dates and duration; design/backend/API responsibilities; live URL or sanitized screenshots; customer permission; test/deployment details; measured result with source; known limitations; and whether it can support a public case study. Until then, project references are portfolio descriptions, not verified client outcomes.
