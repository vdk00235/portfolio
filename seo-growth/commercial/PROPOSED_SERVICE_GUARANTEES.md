# Proposed service guarantees — owner approval required

This document is an internal proposal. It is **not** a customer policy and must not be published until the business owner verifies operational capacity and approves contract language.

## Option set for discussion

| Policy area | Conservative proposed option | Other decision to make |
|---|---|---|
| Defect warranty | 30 calendar days after accepted launch/handover; only reproducible in-scope defects against the signed specification | Choose start event; consider 14/30/60 days by service type; check local law |
| Bug definition | A delivered feature materially fails its agreed acceptance criteria in the documented supported environment | Define severity and reproduction requirements |
| Warranty remedy | Diagnose and correct covered defect at no additional development fee within reasonable agreed effort | Whether workaround/redo/refund applies; legal review |
| Maintenance | Separate opt-in contract after warranty; fixed monthly scope, fee, service hours and overage | Update cadence, backups, monitoring, included hours and security responsibility |
| Support response | Publish only a measured business-hour acknowledgement target after owner confirms staffing and baseline | Channel parity, weekends, holidays and incident escalation |
| New features | Written change request with price and revised timeline, accepted before work | Rate card/fixed quote policy |
| Third-party issues | Excluded from defect warranty where outside delivered code/control; optional paid diagnosis | Define vendor account ownership, provider fees and incident coordination |
| Ownership and handover | State source-code transfer, credentials, documentation and training in the quote; tie transfer conditions to contract/payment | Identify reusable components and third-party licences |
| Security/availability | No uptime/security guarantee without an operational design, monitoring, response coverage and contract | Whether a managed hosting/support service is feasible |

## Suggested warranty clause outline for counsel

“For [approved period] after [approved start event], the provider will investigate and correct reproducible defects in the delivered scope that materially fail to meet the written acceptance criteria in the agreed environment. This does not include new/changed requirements, client or third-party modifications, unsupported environments, failures of external hosting/APIs/payment providers, or routine maintenance unless stated otherwise. The customer must report the issue through [channel] with steps/evidence. Response and remediation targets, where offered, are set out separately in the statement of work.”

This is a drafting outline, not legal advice and not ready to use. Have a local professional adapt it before signing.

## Approval checklist

- Confirm warranty duration, commencement, eligible project types and defect definition.
- Confirm response and fix targets can be met by the people actually available.
- Confirm what testing, documentation, source transfer and training are included.
- Price the labor and infrastructure needed for every proposed support tier.
- Review client-data, security, liability, third-party and legal terms.
- Obtain explicit business-owner approval before changing website, quote templates, structured data or public policy copy.
