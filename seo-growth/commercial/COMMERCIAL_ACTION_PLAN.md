## Decision sequence

**First owner decisions:** business identity/relationship, active services, project evidence, monitored contact and geography. **Then operational work:** lead logging, cost model, delivery baselines, support/warranty costing and legal review. **Then publish:** factual service pages, approved starting prices/policies, case studies and translated routes. This sequence lets the website answer commercial questions without publishing promises the business cannot meet.

## Safe technical improvement selected for this module

The AI-search knowledge file should continue to describe verifiable portfolio content only. `public/llms.txt` has been aligned to the boundary: it does not claim rates, local business address, company legal status, response/delivery SLA, AI offer or warranty. The form no longer reports failed provider delivery as success, and its helper copy avoids unsupported response promises. Full delivery verification still requires a production end-to-end check and any private provider credential to be moved server-side.
