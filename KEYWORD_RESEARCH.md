# Keyword research — SEO Phase 2

**Research date:** 23 November 2026. **Target market:** Chad, with N'Djamena as a priority if the developer's actual service location is confirmed.

## Data quality and limits

No numerical search-volume or competition data was obtained. The phrases below are **keyword suggestions**, built from the current portfolio, language variants and search-result wording. They are hypotheses for Search Console and Keyword Planner validation, not measured demand, and are not ranked by volume. Google describes Keyword Planner's average monthly searches as historical estimates tied to selected location, network and time range; advertiser competition is paid-search competition, not organic ranking difficulty ([Google Ads: keyword metrics](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en)). Before buying or claiming volume, run the list in Keyword Planner for Chad, select French/English/Arabic separately, and preserve the date, network, match/close-variant settings and range. Later use Search Console query and impression data for actual site performance.

Current search-result pages show that phrases such as *développeur mobile*, *développeur Flutter* and full-stack/web/mobile development are used in Chad/N'Djamena hiring and profile contexts. These are observed wording examples, **not volume measurements**: [Salima developer-mobile listing](https://salima-go.com/careers), [PERCHE careers](https://percheservices.com/carrieres), [N'Djamena full-stack listing](https://www.lesopportunites.com/opportunite/tchad/stages/informatique-electronique/avis-de-recrutement-des-stagiaires-en-developpement-web-et-mobiles-android-ios-full-stack-ndjamena-tchad/).

## Practical prioritization (not volume ranking)

1. **Brand and person:** immediately relevant and factual, subject to confirming name transliteration and official profile ownership.
2. **Full-stack, web, mobile, Flutter, React Native, FastAPI, Node.js:** supported by listed skills and portfolio examples; do not promise client service scope until confirmed.
3. **Chad/N'Djamena modifiers:** potentially valuable local intent, but currently unsupported by a stated address or verified service area.
4. **Opica Soft company terms:** wait until the relationship, organization description and active offers are confirmed.
5. **AI terms:** defer unless the developer can point to shipped or substantial original AI work and accurately describe capabilities.

## Validation workflow

- Google Ads Keyword Planner: export separate Chad-targeted suggestions and historical metrics by search language; record the account date, exact location, language, network and range. Treat advertiser competition as paid-ad competition only.
- Google Search Console after verified deployment: review actual queries, country, impressions, clicks and landing-page data; compare Chad and other countries.
- Ask real prospective customers how they describe the need; use those terms naturally in detailed service/project content.
- Revisit the list after enough impressions accrue. Do not fill pages with exact-match repetitions.
